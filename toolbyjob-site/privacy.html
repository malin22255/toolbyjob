Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Who we are

ToolByJob (toolbyjob.site) is an independent directory of AI tools, organized by use case. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it. Questions? Reach us via the contact options on our About page.

2. Data we collect

  • Form submissions. When you submit a tool or sponsorship inquiry via Google Forms, the data you enter (name, email, tool info) is processed by Google on our behalf and used only to review and respond to your submission.
  • Usage analytics. We may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g., Plausible) to understand which pages are visited. Where used, data is aggregated and contains no personal identifiers. If we ever use cookies-based analytics (e.g., Google Analytics), this policy will be updated and a consent notice added where required.
  • Local storage. We store your language preference (English/Chinese) in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.
  • We do not require an account or collect data beyond what the forms explicitly ask for.

4. Third-party links

We link to external tools. Once you leave this site, their own privacy policies apply. We encourage you to review them.

5. Sponsored placements

Sponsored listings are clearly disclosed as such and are kept separate from our editorial recommendations. Sponsored placement never changes the editorial "why it fits" assessment of other tools on a page.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., EU/EEA under GDPR), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you submitted through our forms. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, note them on the homepage.
