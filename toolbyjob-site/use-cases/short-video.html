How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Start from one long recording从一条长视频开始

A podcast, webinar, livestream or 10-minute talking-head video is raw material for 5–10 clips.播客、直播、讲座或 10 分钟口播，都能切出 5–10 条短片。

2

Auto-generate clips自动生成切片

Run it through OpusClip. Review the suggested hooks, keep the best 3–5.丢给 OpusClip，看它建议的开头钩子，留下最好的 3–5 条。

3

Add captions and post vertical加字幕、发竖版

Polish in CapCut with captions and a hook in the first 2 seconds. Post to Shorts / Reels / Douyin.在 CapCut 加字幕，前 2 秒放钩子，然后发 Shorts / Reels / 抖音。

FAQ常见问题

How many shorts can I get from one long video?一条长视频能切几条短片？ +

Typically 5–10 from a 30–60 minute recording. Quality beats quantity: publish the 3 with the strongest hooks.30–60 分钟的素材通常能切 5–10 条，但宁缺毋滥，优先发钩子最强的 3 条。

Are the auto-captions accurate?自动字幕准确吗？ +

Around 95%+ for clear English and Chinese speech. Always skim and fix names and jargon before posting.清晰的英文和中文大约 95% 以上准确，发布前记得检查人名和专业词。

Is CapCut free for commercial use?CapCut 可以商用吗？ +

The free tier covers most needs; some templates and effects require CapCut Pro. Check the license for ads-heavy commercial use.免费版覆盖大部分需求，部分模板特效需要 Pro；重度商业投放前确认一下授权条款。
