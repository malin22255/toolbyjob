How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Write your offer in one sentence把卖点写成一句话

“X helps [audience] achieve [result] without [pain].” If you cannot, the page will not work.「X 帮助（人群）实现（结果），不用（痛苦）」。写不出来，页面也救不了。

2

Generate, then sharpen the hero生成页面，再打磨首屏

Let AI build the draft; then hand-edit the headline and first screenshot yourself.让 AI 出草稿，然后亲手改标题和首屏配图——这两处决定转化。

3

Publish and buy tiny traffic上线并买一点点流量

$20 of ads or 3 relevant communities is enough to see if anyone clicks “Sign up”.$20 广告或发 3 个相关社群，就能看出有没有人点「注册」。
