How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Let AI join every call让 AI 参加每一次会

Connect Fathom or Otter to Zoom/Meet once. Everyone gets the transcript automatically.把 Fathom 或 Otter 接入 Zoom/Meet 一次，之后转写全自动。

2

Extract decisions and owners提取决议和负责人

Ask: “List decisions made, tasks with owners and deadlines, open questions.”提问：「列出已达成的决议、带负责人和期限的任务、未决问题」。

3

Push tasks where work happens把任务推进到干活的地方

Action items go to Notion / Linear / email within 10 minutes of the meeting ending.散会 10 分钟内，把待办推进 Notion / Linear / 邮件。

FAQ常见问题

Is it legal to record meetings?录会议合法吗？ +

Get consent — most tools announce recording automatically. Check local law and your company policy first.要先获得同意，多数工具会自动播报录音提示；先查当地法律和公司政策。

Which tool is actually free?哪个真的免费？ +

Fathom’s core product is free with unlimited meetings — the best starting point. Otter free gives 300 min/month.Fathom 核心功能免费且不限会议数，最佳起点；Otter 免费版每月 300 分钟。

Can it handle Chinese meetings?中文会议支持吗？ +

Otter and Fireflies handle Mandarin reasonably well; always skim proper nouns and numbers in the summary.Otter 和 Fireflies 对普通话支持不错，总结里的人名和数字记得核对。
