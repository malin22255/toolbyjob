How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Write the script for the ear按「听觉」写脚本

Short sentences. Read it aloud once — if you stumble, the listener will too.短句。自己读一遍，你卡壳的地方听众也会卡。

2

Pick a voice and tune it选音色并微调

Match voice energy to content: calm for tutorials, upbeat for promos. Adjust speed ±10%.音色能量要配内容：教程沉稳、促销明快，语速微调 ±10%。

3

Export and sync to video导出并与视频对齐

Export WAV, drop into CapCut/Descript, align to cuts, then add background music at low volume.导出 WAV，丢进 CapCut/Descript 对齐剪辑点，配乐音量调低。

FAQ常见问题

Can I clone my own voice?能克隆自己的声音吗？ +

Yes with ElevenLabs — a short sample is enough. Only clone your own voice or voices you have written permission to use.ElevenLabs 可以，一小段样本就够；只能克隆自己的声音或有书面授权的声音。

Does AI voice sound robotic?AI 配音还很机械吗？ +

Top-tier voices pass casual listening easily. Add punctuation and breaks — that is what makes them breathe.一线音色日常听感已过关；标点和停顿写好，听起来就自然。

Which languages work best?哪些语言效果最好？ +

English, Chinese, Spanish and German are excellent. Test shorter scripts in less common languages first.英、中、西、德语很成熟；小语种先用短脚本试听再批量生产。
