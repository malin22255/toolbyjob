FAQ常见问题

What open rate should I expect?打开率多少算正常？ +

40%+ for a well-run welcome series, 25–35% for weekly newsletters to engaged lists.运营良好的欢迎序列 40%+；活跃列表的周更 newsletter 约 25–35%。

Which platform for a beginner?新手选哪个平台？ +

Beehiiv for newsletters/newsletter business; Mailchimp if you also run an ecommerce shop.纯内容订阅选 Beehiiv；带电商店铺选 Mailchimp。

How do I avoid the spam folder?怎么避免进垃圾箱？ +

Warm up gradually, authenticate your domain (SPF/DKIM), and email only people who explicitly opted in.逐步预热发信量、配好域名验证（SPF/DKIM）、只发给明确订阅的人。
