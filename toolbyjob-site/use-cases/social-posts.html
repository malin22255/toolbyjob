How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Write down one idea in one sentence把想法写成一句话

Example: “We cut our onboarding time in half.” One idea feeds a whole week of content.例如：「我们把上手时间缩短了一半」。一个想法够喂一整周的内容。

2

Ask AI for platform versions让 AI 生成各平台版本

Prompt: “Turn this into a LinkedIn post (150 words), a tweet thread, and a Xiaohongshu note with emojis.”提示词：「把这句话改成 LinkedIn 帖（150 词）、推文串、带 emoji 的小红书笔记」。

3

Schedule a week at once一次性排期一周

Paste everything into Buffer, set the week, done. Batch on Monday, forget about it.全部粘进 Buffer，设定一周排期。周一批量做完，之后不用管。
