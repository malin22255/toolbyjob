FAQ常见问题

Will customers notice it is AI?顾客会介意是 AI 吗？ +

They notice quality, not identity. Disclose “AI assistant” honestly and make escape to a human effortless.顾客在意的是回答质量。诚实标注 AI 助手，并让人工转接毫无阻力。

How much does AI support cost?AI 客服要多少钱？ +

Tidio starts free; Lyro AI from about $39/mo. Intercom Fin is about $0.99 per resolution — pay per solved ticket.Tidio 有免费版，Lyro 约 $39/月起；Intercom Fin 按解决量收费约 $0.99/次。

What should never be automated?哪些不该自动化？ +

Refunds, legal issues, pricing exceptions and anything with real anger. Automate the repetitive, humanize the sensitive.退款、法律、特殊定价和真生气的客户。重复的交给 AI，敏感的留给人。
