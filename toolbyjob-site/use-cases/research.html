How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Define one specific question把问题定义得足够具体

“Should we enter the EU market?” is too big. “What VAT rules apply to selling X in Germany?” works.「该不该进欧洲市场」太大，「在德国卖 X 适用什么增值税规则」才可用。

2

Ask for sources, not just answers要来源，不只答案

Prompt: “Answer with citations to primary sources; flag anything you are unsure about.”提示词：「回答需引用一手来源；不确定的部分要标注」。

3

Verify the 3 claims that matter核查最关键的 3 个论断

Click through citations for any claim you will act on. AI still hallucinates occasionally.凡是你要据此行动的论断，点开引用核实。AI 偶尔还是会编。

FAQ常见问题

Do AI tools still make up sources?还会编造来源吗？ +

Much less with search-grounded tools like Perplexity, but it happens. Treat citations as a lead, not proof.Perplexity 这类搜索型工具少多了，但仍会发生。引用是线索，不是证据。

Perplexity free vs Pro?Perplexity 免费版够吗？ +

Free covers quick questions; Pro ($20/mo) adds more powerful models, file uploads and Deep Research quota.快速问答免费版够用；Pro（$20/月）有更强模型、文件上传和深度研究额度。

How do I research Chinese sources?怎么查中文资料？ +

Combine Perplexity with Chinese search engines and verify on primary sites; coverage of Chinese web sources varies.Perplexity 结合中文搜索引擎使用，重要信息回原始站点核实；中文网页源覆盖并不稳定。
