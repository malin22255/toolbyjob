How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Pick 5 real competitors选 5 个真竞品

2 direct, 2 adjacent, 1 “status quo” (what customers do instead of buying anything).2 个直接、2 个相邻、1 个「现状替代」（用户不买你时用什么凑合）。

2

Gather the same 5 facts for each给每家收集同样的 5 项事实

Positioning line, pricing, main channel, top content, biggest weakness. Same template for all.定位语、定价、主要获客渠道、头部内容、最大短板——全部用同一模板。

3

Summarize gaps into one table把缺口总结成一张表

The money is in the empty cells: what none of them offer is your positioning.空格就是机会：没人提供的东西，就是你的定位。

FAQ常见问题

How accurate is Similarweb data?Similarweb 数据准吗？ +

Directionally useful, not exact. Use it to compare trends between sites, not absolute numbers.方向性有用，别当精确值。适合比较站点间的趋势，不适合抠绝对数。

How do I track competitors continuously?怎么持续追踪竞品？ +

Subscribe to their newsletters, follow their changelogs, and set a monthly 1-hour review with AI summarizing what changed.订他们的邮件、关注更新日志，每月花 1 小时让 AI 总结变化。

Can AI read competitor pricing pages?AI 能读竞品定价页吗？ +

Yes — paste the URL content or screenshots into Claude/ChatGPT and ask for a structured comparison table.可以，把页面内容或截图贴给 Claude/ChatGPT，让它输出结构化对比表。
