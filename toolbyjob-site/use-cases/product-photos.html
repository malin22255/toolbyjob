How to do it in 3 steps三步做完

1

Shoot one clean base photo拍一张干净的基础图

Phone + white wall + daylight is enough. The AI builds everything else around it.手机 + 白墙 + 自然光就够，其他交给 AI。

2

Generate scenes and variations生成场景和变体

Place the product in a kitchen, on a desk, in a hand — generate 3 scenes per product.把产品放进厨房、书桌、手里——每个产品生成 3 个场景。

3

Resize for each channel按渠道改尺寸

One 1:1 master, then export story, feed and ad sizes from Canva.做一张 1:1 母版，再用 Canva 导出故事、信息流和广告各种尺寸。

FAQ常见问题

Can AI really replace a product photoshoot?AI 能完全替代产品摄影吗？ +

For social and listing backgrounds, yes. For hero images where fine texture matters, a real photo is still safer.社媒和详情页背景可以；对质感要求极高的主图，实拍仍更稳。

Which tool is safe for commercial ads?哪个工具商用最安全？ +

Adobe Firefly is designed for commercial use with licensed training data. Check each platform’s terms for ad campaigns.Adobe Firefly 用授权数据训练、面向商用设计；投放广告前核对各平台条款。

How do I keep my product accurate in AI images?怎么保证产品不被 AI 画歪？ +

Use image-to-image with your real photo as the base, then inpaint only the background — never regenerate the product itself.用图生图、以实拍图为基底，只重绘背景，绝不重新生成产品本身。
