FAQ常见问题

Will Google penalize AI product copy?谷歌会惩罚 AI 文案吗？ +

No — Google ranks on quality and helpfulness, not on how text was drafted. Unique, specific copy ranks fine.不会。谷歌看的是质量和有用性，不是谁写的。独特、具体的文案照样有排名。

How do I keep a consistent brand voice?怎么保持品牌语气一致？ +

Paste 2–3 examples of your best copy into the prompt as style references, or use Jasper brand voice.把你最好的 2–3 段旧文案贴进提示词当风格参考，或用 Jasper 的品牌声音功能。

Can I write in multiple languages?能写多语言吗？ +

Yes — all four tools handle Chinese/English translation-style rewriting well. Have a native speaker spot-check the top pages.可以，四个工具的中英改写都不错，重点页面建议母语者抽查一遍。
